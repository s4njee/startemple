EXPERIMENT 02 / GRAVITY

EventHorizon.

A black hole with a disc of gas round it, every pixel's light followed back past it through Einstein's gravity. The far side of the disc is bent up over the top of the hole and under the bottom, the side spinning towards you burns brighter and bluer, and the stars behind are pulled round the shadow into arcs.

Move the cursor to fly round the hole: along the disc to swing round it, across the disc to rise over it or dip beneath.

WHAT THE LIGHT GOES THROUGH

Every ray,
bent.

01 / GEODESICS

Light that falls.

Each pixel sends a ray back into the scene and steps it through the curved space round the hole, hundreds of small steps, pulled harder the closer it passes. A ray that comes within one and a half times the horizon's radius can orbit; closer still, it falls in, and that pixel is black: the shadow.

02 / THE DISC

Brighter coming towards you.

The gas orbits at a large share of the speed of light. Coming towards you its light is squeezed bluer and beamed brighter; going away, redder and dimmer. Near the inner edge, the light loses energy climbing out of the hole's well, and dims.

03 / THE RING

The far side, over the top.

Light from the disc behind the hole is bent up over it and down under it, so the disc seems to wrap the shadow. The thin bright line hugging the shadow is light that went round the hole before it escaped: the photon ring.
