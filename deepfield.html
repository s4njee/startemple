EXPERIMENT 01 / THE HUBBLE PALETTE

GalaxyBrain.

A cloud of glowing gas, drawn the way the famous space telescope pictures are: three colours of light that the eye can barely tell apart, each given a primary of its own. The cloud churns slowly, its colours turn round the wheel, and thousands of stars drift in front of it in four layers, twinkling.

01 / EMISSION

Gas glows in lines.

Hot gas does not shine in every colour. Sulphur glows at 672 nm, hydrogen at 656 nm, both deep red, and oxygen at 501 nm, teal. The Hubble palette paints them red, green and blue, so gas the eye would see as one red shows its chemistry.

02 / MORPH

Noise through time.

The gas is layers of warped noise, and the dust lanes are another warped field that only absorbs. Giving the noise a third axis of time makes the cloud churn. It is redrawn every frame at a third of the screen's size, which its softness hides.

03 / STARS

Light that holds still.

Each star is a soft spot spread over the pixels around it, so it keeps its brightness wherever it sits between them, and does not crawl as it drifts. The twinkle is put back on purpose: each star follows its own slow flicker, never faster than three swings a second.
